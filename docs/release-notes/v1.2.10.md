# Keepory v1.2.10

Updates and improvements since v1.2.9.

## English

- Restored Naver Blog previews, including post titles and cover images.
- Shared TikTok short links now show the video title and thumbnail again.
- AI suggestions skip bookmarked pages that are in Trash or archived, and
  re-check account ownership, so no suggestions are generated or retried for a
  bookmark you can no longer see.
- The installed web app and notification prompts now say Keepory consistently.

## 한국어

- 네이버 블로그 미리보기를 복원해 게시물 제목과 대표 이미지가 표시됩니다.
- 공유한 틱톡 짧은 링크의 제목과 썸네일이 다시 표시됩니다.
- AI 추천이 휴지통·보관한 북마크를 건너뛰고 계정 소유권을 다시 확인해, 보이지
  않는 북마크에 대해 생성이나 재시도를 하지 않습니다.
- 웹 앱 설치 화면과 알림 문구의 앱 이름을 Keepory로 통일했습니다.

## Provenance

Includes #965 (Naver Blog metadata and cover images, STASH-7K), #966 (TikTok
shortlink previews, STASH-7V/STASH-7T), #977 (P1: block inactive AI enrichment
and verify ownership boundaries), #975 (Keepory rename across the PWA manifest,
notification copy, and the link-preview fetcher URL), plus dependency patches
#968, #969, and #972. #971 and #988 net out to no user-visible change.
Android uses an EAS production AAB; the sideload APK is a separate artifact.
